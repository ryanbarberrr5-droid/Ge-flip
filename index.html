Where the numbers come from

Prices come from the OSRS Wiki real-time price feed, which collects completed Grand Exchange trades from RuneLite users. Buy is the latest insta-sell price (what a buy offer at that price fills at). Sell is the latest insta-buy price. Trades/hr is the number of items traded in the last hour on both sides.

The tax

The GE takes 2% of the sell price on every completed sale, rounded down per item, capped at 5,000,000 gp per item. Items that sell for under 50 gp pay nothing because 2% rounds down to zero. Bonds are exempt. Every margin and profit figure on this page already has the tax removed.

Margin = Sell − Buy − min(floor(Sell × 0.02), 5,000,000)

Profit / 4h

Margin multiplied by how many you can realistically move in one buy-limit window (4 hours). That quantity is the smallest of three caps: the buy limit, what your cash stack covers, and your share of the hourly volume (the slower side of the last hour's trades × 4 × the share you set). The small text under each figure says which cap applied. The volume cap is what stops an item with a giant spread and three trades an hour from topping the list. Set it to "Ignore volume" to see pure buy-limit numbers.

Flags

  • Swing: the latest trade is more than 15% away from the hourly average on either side. Often a single odd trade or someone manipulating the price. Verify before committing.
  • Stale: the older of the two prices is past your max age. The margin may no longer exist.
  • Slow: fewer items trade per hour than a quarter of the buy limit, so filling the full limit will take a long time.
  • No tax: tax rounds to zero or the item is exempt.

Before you commit gold

Margin-check in game: buy one at the Sell price, then sell it at the Buy price. If the real margin matches, scale up. Hourly volume and age tell you whether a margin is real; a huge margin on an item that trades twice a day is usually not.

How this list works

Every method comes from the OSRS Wiki's money-making guides (539 methods, checked 1 Oct 2026). Where a guide's recipe is made of tradeable items, the page prices it live from the Wiki price feed (untradeable parts such as charges and crystal dust are valued from the items they come from, as the Wiki does) and every figure has GE tax removed. Where the guide relies on drop tables, charges or shop-only items, the Wiki's own hourly estimate is shown instead and marked Wiki est.

Profit / hr sorts live numbers and Wiki estimates together so the whole list ranks at once. Wiki / hr is the Wiki's figure for every method, useful as a sanity check against a live number.

  • Instant buys inputs at the insta-buy price and sells outputs at the insta-sell price. Patient is the reverse. The 1-hour average basis smooths odd trades.
  • Market cap limits your rate to a share of the last hour's trade volume for the main items, so a product that trades 20 times an hour cannot show a million an hour.
  • Difficulty adds up the highest required level, every quest (Novice to Grandmaster, longer quests count more, started-only quests half, recommended quests a quarter), click intensity, dangerous areas, and extra unlocks such as diaries, kudos or a house lectern. One bar is trivial, five is hard. Hover or tap the bar for the breakdown.
  • Level is the highest level the guide requires. Levels the guide only recommends are shown in grey and don't lock a method unless you switch that on under My levels.
  • Daily methods (shops with a daily stock, farming runs, Miscellania) show profit per hour of your active time, the way the Wiki lists them, with the per-run profit and how often you can repeat it underneath.
  • Methods with several products (jewellery, dyes, amulets) show whichever piece pays best right now, within the levels you entered.
