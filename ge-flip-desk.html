Where the numbers come from

Prices come from the OSRS Wiki real-time price feed, which collects completed Grand Exchange trades from RuneLite users. Buy is the latest insta-sell price (what a buy offer at that price fills at). Sell is the latest insta-buy price. Trades/hr is the number of items traded in the last hour on both sides.

The tax

The GE takes 2% of the sell price on every completed sale, rounded down per item, capped at 5,000,000 gp per item. Items that sell for under 50 gp pay nothing because 2% rounds down to zero. Bonds are exempt. Every margin and profit figure on this page already has the tax removed.

Margin = Sell − Buy − min(floor(Sell × 0.02), 5,000,000)

Profit / 4h

Margin multiplied by how many you can realistically move in one buy-limit window (4 hours). That quantity is the smallest of three caps: the buy limit, what your cash stack covers, and your share of the hourly volume (the slower side of the last hour's trades × 4 × the share you set). The small text under each figure says which cap applied. The volume cap is what stops an item with a giant spread and three trades an hour from topping the list. Set it to "Ignore volume" to see pure buy-limit numbers.

Flags

  • Swing: the latest trade is more than 15% away from the hourly average on either side. Often a single odd trade or someone manipulating the price. Verify before committing.
  • Stale: the older of the two prices is past your max age. The margin may no longer exist.
  • Slow: fewer items trade per hour than a quarter of the buy limit, so filling the full limit will take a long time.
  • No tax: tax rounds to zero or the item is exempt.

Before you commit gold

Margin-check in game: buy one at the Sell price, then sell it at the Buy price. If the real margin matches, scale up. Hourly volume and age tell you whether a margin is real; a huge margin on an item that trades twice a day is usually not.
